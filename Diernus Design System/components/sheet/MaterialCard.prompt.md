One-line: a material spec card — CSS-painted texture swatch on top, code/name/notes below, supplier ref under a dashed rule.

```jsx
<MaterialCard code="A2 · FBF-24" name="Zak+Fox · Bayan" texture="velvet" supplierRef="REF. ZFBY-10 (BLUE-10)">
  Veludo azul da almofada de assento fixa.
</MaterialCard>
```

Notes: the three built-in textures (burl, velvet, stripe) are the ones drawn in the source. Supply your own CSS background for a new material rather than a photo.
